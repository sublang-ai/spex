# T03 — Meridian Bank Digital

## Who they are

The digital-channels group of a 2,000-person bank; Java; SAFe with a change advisory board.

## Day 2: procedures

`propose` (plan with a risk class and rollback, reviewed by a Risk Officer); `release` (deploy gate with an approval question). Built-ins `decide`, `code`, `review`; adopts T10's `rollout`.

## Day 3: project

a change in a Spring sample application added as an existing clone.

## What happened

- **Day 2.** `propose` was described in chat: an analyst drafts an intent record with a risk class, a risk officer reviews it, the record is committed on agreement. The first compile failed at the machine step because the role was named in two words ("Risk Officer", W-12); renamed to `RiskOfficer`, it compiled, and registration bound Analyst to `dev.analyst` and RiskOfficer to `dev.reviewer`.
- **Day 3.** `propose-lab` was created from the palette and paired into the team's subgroup. `/propose` on the pending-card-transactions request produced IR-001 (medium risk) in one commit after a review with no findings; two product choices were flagged for sign-off. `/decide` recorded DR-001 (query the feed on each request) after seven proposal/review rounds, with acceptance gated on three confirmations from the feed's owner. `/code` for IR-001 first asked which scope to deliver (the plan assumed the bank's real systems; the lab repository had none) and, told to build a standalone Java 21 service with in-memory stubs, rewrote the plan and delivered it in four commits with 13 tests, every review round clean. `/rollout` (T10's, from the registry) first asked whether to spec deployment or write mock deploy files (mocks), then how to watch two rollback signals the service does not emit (outside-visible stand-ins), wrote and dry-ran the plan (`bc87bcc`, `6e075a2`), passed the SRE's second round, and asked the owner whether to start; answered no, the step that reads the answer failed the run with "an unexpected extra detail" (W-23's third shape). Stopped; the plan stays as the record. After Playbook 17.8.0 was adopted and T10 republished `rollout` 0.1.1, T03's environment picked the new version up on resolve and the plan was re-run: the engineer asked which commit to target, wrote and dry-ran the plan, the SRE found nothing, and the owner's "No" ended the run as rejected with no failure.
- **Sharing.** `propose` was published to the workshop org after the org and license edits every team needed (W-18, W-21); the package was re-requested under its new name.
- **Release.** `release` was described in chat: a releaser assembles an evidence bundle (the tests run and recorded, the rollout plan and its approval line, a rollback plan from the plan's commands, a release note naming the risk class) and commits it; the board's approver reviews in up to two rounds; then the owner is asked "Approve the release of <change>?". The agent asked one question (findings left after round two: end held, never ask) and compiled on the first attempt in 13 minutes; Releaser → `dev.coder`, Approver → `dev.reviewer`; published as `claude-workshop-261011/release@0.1.0` (package commit `64b7f4c`). `/release` asked which change (HEAD was the package commit; answered `66e9620`, IR-001), wrote the bundle (`ef39cd4`) with the tests unrun because the machine had only JDK 19, took the approver's one finding, fetched a JDK 21 itself and recorded 13/13 passing (`237f682`), passed round two and, on the owner's yes, ended "The release of 66e9620 is approved … The approved evidence bundle is in docs/releases/66e9620/" — without the commit the playbook reported (W-32). The Releaser had first read the playbook's own exported skill and told the Boss, falsely, that the review and approval "won't run automatically" (W-34). T05 adopted `release` from the registry.

## What they found

- W-12: a two-word role name passes two compile phases and fails at the third with the role read as its first word.
- The built-in `/decide` ran seven proposal/review rounds for one decision; the Captain's "saved you 14 interruptions" line reads as a cost here, not a saving.
