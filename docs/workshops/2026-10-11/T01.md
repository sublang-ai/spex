# T01 — Lumen Labs

## Who they are

A six-person SaaS startup on a Kanban board with continuous deploys; TypeScript. Planning and design merged into one sitting.

## Day 2: procedures

`shape` (plan + design in one): a founder request becomes one reviewed intent record and its spec items, committed once. Built-ins `code`, `review` for the rest; adopts T08's `evaluate`.

## Day 3: project

`invite-codes`: a waitlist and invite-code HTTP API, created in Spex.

## What happened

- **Day 2.** `shape` was described in chat: an analyst turns a founder request into one intent record with spec items and acceptance checks, a reviewer runs up to two rounds, one commit at the end. Compiled on the first attempt once roles were single words; registration bound Analyst to `dev.analyst` and Reviewer to `dev.reviewer`; the coder lane is GPT-6.1 Sol at xhigh.
- **Day 3.** `invite-codes` was created from the palette (TypeScript). `/shape` was interrupted by a core restart (the Homebrew hang, W-16) and resumed with Retry; it asked the founder two policy questions, then finished in one commit (`24b1d9c`) with two late fixes no reviewer saw, as the playbook allows. The session still reads as failed in the sidebar (W-22). The built-in `/code` then delivered IR-001 with a GPT-6.1 Sol coder and an Opus reviewer: ten commits over nine review rounds, 74 tests passing, a README; the run took about 75 minutes. T08's `/evaluate`, added from the registry, then evaluated the service against four thresholds in one evaluator commit and one clean review round: 74/74 tests, zero over-admissions in a 1,000-request replay, exactly one of 50 concurrent redemptions succeeding, p95 0.137 ms — the whole planned chain `shape → code → review → evaluate` walked.
- **Sharing.** `shape` was published to the workshop org; T04 and T10 added it from the registry.
- **On the rebuilt build (2026-10-12), through the page.** Groups listed the workshop group and every team under it by name and path, with nothing of the backend; the built-ins read "17.8.1 shipped with this app; this environment runs 17.8.0" and one Update moved them (W-30). History on the Dashboard still reads "Nothing done here yet" for the three finished runs, because they were chat turns, not ledger intents (W-49).
