# App 0.9.0-beta.5 release preparation

Prepared 2026-10-01 for `app-v0.9.0-beta.5`, the fifth beta on the way to 0.9.0 ([DR-087](../../specs/decisions/087-beta-app-releases.md)), covering desktop and server as source.
A beta gates on CI, the smoke and the live smoke, and runs neither the regression nor the manual checklist ([release-26](../../specs/packages/release.md#release-26)).
The [release rules](../../specs/packages/release.md) remain the gate authority.

## Review

Since `app-v0.9.0-beta.4` the range adds the beta's publication record, one record correction, and one merge of change: the compiler adopted ([DR-093](../../specs/decisions/093-a-players-subagent-model.md), [DR-092](../../specs/decisions/092-playbook-17-1-slc-0-13-cligent-0-28-adoption.md)):

- Both shells require slc `^0.14.0`, the release that declares Cligent `^0.29.0` and Playbook `^17.2.0`, so the root override of slc's Cligent range retires and one engine and one Cligent serve the app and its compiler by the dependencies alone.

The changelog's `[Unreleased]` section carries the entries; as for every beta, it gains no section of its own, so this beta's notes are that section at the tag, the earlier betas' notes included ([release-27](../../specs/packages/release.md#release-27)).

## Upgrade boundaries

Those of `app-v0.9.0-beta.4` stand ([its record](0.9.0-beta.4-preparation.md)), less the override it needed: slc 0.14 declares the app's own Cligent and Playbook.
