# DR-227: Remove the Storage Footer from Space

## Status

Accepted (2026-11-08) on the owner's review of the home-directory line at the foot of Space.
Amends [DR-172](172-surfaces-a-fresh-user-reads.md).
Amended by [DR-246](246-remove-groups-and-team-servers-from-the-clients.md).

## Context

The footer named Spex's internal storage folder without offering an action for the projects or records shown above it.
The owner questioned its place in the page.

## Decision

- Amends [DR-172](172-surfaces-a-fresh-user-reads.md) in Space's footer presentation.
- Remove the home-directory footer from Space.
- Records inspection and repair present their own path context.

## Consequences

Space ends with its project list and their task controls.
The surface requirements and their verification follow that presentation.
