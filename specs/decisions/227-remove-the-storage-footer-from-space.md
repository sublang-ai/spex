# DR-227: Remove the Storage Footer from Space

## Status

Accepted (2026-11-08) on the owner's review of the home-directory line beneath Space's groups.
Amends [DR-172](172-surfaces-a-fresh-user-reads.md) in Space's footer presentation.

## Context

The footer names Spex's internal storage folder without offering an action for the groups or records shown above it.
The owner questioned its place in the page.

## Decision

Remove the home-directory footer from Space at this computer and at server places.
Records inspection and repair present their own path context.

## Consequences

Space ends with its groups and their task controls.
The surface requirements and local and server-place verification follow that presentation.
